Client-side [Fabric](https://fabricmc.net/) utility mod for the **Telos Realms** Minecraft server, with a fully
animated ClickGUI, movable HUD elements and many QoL features.



Discord: https://discord.gg/dqjD94x3Ra

## Controls

- **Right Shift** to open the ClickGUI (rebindable in Controls → *Telemetric*).
- Panel header: **left-drag** to move, **right-click** to collapse.
- Module row: **left-click** to toggle, the **gear** opens its Settings window (sections on the left, drag the title to move, edges to resize, ☆ pins a setting), **right-click** shows the bind and your pinned settings.
- **Misc → HUD → Edit HUD** then drag to move, corner/scroll to resize, **M** to mirror, **Esc** when done.

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) **0.20.1+** for **Minecraft 26.1.2**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and `telemetric-<version>.jar` into `.minecraft/mods`.
3. Requires **Java 25**.
